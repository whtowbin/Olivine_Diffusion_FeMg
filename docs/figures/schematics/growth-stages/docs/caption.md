# Caption

**Fig. X | A zoned profile can record several growth events, and modelling them as growth
stages lets each zone diffuse only from the time it grew.**
In FORSTER a crystal can grow in instantaneous stages. Stage 0 is the starting profile.
At the start of each later stage the rim moves outward and a new zone with its own Fo
appears; from then on it diffuses together with everything grown before it. Inner zones
have therefore diffused for longer than outer ones. Only model times after the last stage
can fit the measured profile, and FORSTER reports both the time since the first stage and
the time since the last stage. Stage start times are fixed inputs.

**a**, Stage 0: a crystal of Fo 0.90 whose rim (40 µm inside the final rim) is held at
Fo 0.86 for 6 days. The dashed line is the starting profile (t = 0) and the blue line the
model after 6 days; the outer 40 µm has not grown yet. **b**, Stage 1 at 6 days: the rim
moves out to its final position and a new zone of Fo 0.82 appears, held at Fo 0.82 at the
rim. Its inner contact is a sharp, undiffused step on top of a gradient that has already
diffused for 6 days. **c**, The measured profile at 10 days (grey circles) and the staged
model's best fit (blue). The outer step has diffused for 4 days, the inner gradient for
10 days. **d**, Misfit (χ², log scale) against time since stage 0. The staged model has
no fit before stage 1, because the outer zone does not exist yet; its best fit is at
10.1 days (1σ 9.9-10.3), which FORSTER reports as 10.1 days since the first stage and
4.1 days since the last. Fitting the same profile with both zones present from time zero
(grey dashed) gives 6.7 days (1σ 6.1-7.5), which is neither of the two times. Its misfit
is just as low (χ² 64.9 against 66.0 for 66 points), so the fit alone cannot tell the two
histories apart: the growth history has to come from petrography, other elements or
zoning that only growth explains. Circled letters mark the moments shown in **a**-**c**.

Illustrative case, computed with FORSTER's own model (the case of its growth-stage
tests): 1200 °C, 42 MPa, fO2 1.38 × 10^-3 Pa, profile along [001], Dohmen & Chakraborty
(2007) diffusivity. The "measured" profile is synthetic (66 points from an independent
solver at 10 days, noise 0.001 Fo). The crystal sections are coloured by the computed
profile as if every face had the same profile (real diffusion is faster along c, so real
zones differ in width by direction); the crystal outline is generic.
