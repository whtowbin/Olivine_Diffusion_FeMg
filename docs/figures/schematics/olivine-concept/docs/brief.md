# Brief: the zoned-olivine concept (FORSTER methods paper)

- **Mode:** design (research note `FORSTER_FACTS.md`, no reference figure).
- **Format:** journal, double column, 183 mm wide, about 72 mm high. Arial 5-7 pt, panel letters 8 pt bold.
- **Reader:** geoscientists (petrologists, volcanologists) reading the FORSTER methods paper; new to the project's vocabulary.
- **Style:** own choice: clean flat Nature-style vector, petrology register (a polished section seen from above, line plots with no invented numbers). One olivine green for the crystal, one hue per direction (a-axis, c-axis) carried from the section into the plots, grey for melt and context.

## Takeaway (one sentence)
The relaxed width of a growth step, read with the right D for that direction, is a clock.

## Key ideas (user's criteria first, verbatim from the request)
1. "A zoned olivine crystal in contact with melt, with a microprobe traverse from the rim inward."
2. "The crystallographic axes a [100], b [010], c [001] and the profile's direction relative to them."
3. "Fe-Mg diffusion being faster along c (about 6×)."
4. "A step in Fo relaxing over time (t0 step → later smoothed profiles), so the measured profile's width records D × t."
5. The D along a profile combines the three axis values by its direction (cos² of the angles to a, b, c), so the same time leaves a wider profile along c than along a.

## Must not imply
- A specific crystal habit or face indices (habit unknown: generic outline, no face labels).
- Real Fo values, distances or times (all curves illustrative; no numeric ticks).
- That diffusion has a single isotropic D, or that D along b differs from D along a (the note gives a and b both about 6× slower than c).
- That the profile width depends on D or t alone (it depends on D × t).
- That the step positions must be the same in every direction (they are held equal in panel b only to isolate D; real zone widths differ by face).

## Scope
This figure is the concept only: what a profile records and why direction matters. The fitting (misfit curve, noise model), starting-profile segments beyond one step, growth stages and joint fits belong to later figures and the caption.
